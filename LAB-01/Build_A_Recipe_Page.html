Classic New York Cheesecake!

Welcome to the foolproof recipe of the Classic New York Cheesecake!❤︎⁠

This creamy and rich treat is perfect for satisfying your sweet craving without overindulging. Follow this masterpiece of a recipe to create delicious and mouth watering of a dessert that everyone will love.

Ingredients

The Crust:

  • 1 ½ cups Graham cracker crumbs
  • ⅓ cup Unsalted Butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar

The Filling:

  • 32 oz Creamcheese, softened
  • 1 ¼ cups Sugar
  • 1/2 cup Sour cream, full-fat
  • 3 whole Eggs, 2 Egg yolks
  • 2 teaspoons Pure vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons of All-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon freshly grated lemon zest

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C)
  2. In a bowl, combine the ingredients for the crust./li>
  3. Pack the crust mixture firmly into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan
  4. Bake for 10 minutes, then remove and let cool completely.
  5. Reduce your oven heat to 160°C (325°F).
  6. In a bowl, beat softened cream cheese and sugar on medium speed until smooth.
  7. Mix in flour and lemon zest just until combined.
  8. Blend in the sour cream and vanilla extract until smooth.
  9. Whisk in the eggs and yolks one at a time on low speed.
  10. Pour batter into the pan. Place it inside a large roasting pan and pour boiling water half-way into the roaster.
  11. Bake at 160°C (325°F) for 75 to 90 minutes. It is done when the edges are set but the center still jiggles slightly.
  12. Remove the cake, let it cool completely on the counter, then refrigerate for at least 6 hours.
  13. Slice it and serve. ENJOY!
