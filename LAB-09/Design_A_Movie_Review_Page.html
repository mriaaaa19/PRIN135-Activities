Movie Review: From Up On Poppy Hill (コクリコ坂から)

From Up on Poppy Hill (2011) is a heartwarming Studio Ghibli coming-of-age drama directed by Gorō Miyazaki and written by Hayao Miyazaki. Set in 1963 Yokohama, Japan, the film follows high school student Umi, who raises signal flags every morning in memory of her lost father, and Shun, an energetic student journalist. Together, they join forces with their classmates to save a historic, chaotic school clubhouse from demolition amidst Japan's post-war modernization drive, leading them to uncover shared secrets about their pasts.
